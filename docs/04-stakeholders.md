## 4. Stakeholders

UrbanPulse is designed to support multiple stakeholder groups with different data requirements and decision-making needs.

### 4.1 Operations Team

The Operations Team is responsible for monitoring transportation services and responding to operational issues.

They require near-real-time information about:

- active vehicles;
- vehicle locations;
- route delays;
- average journey times;
- traffic conditions;
- congestion hotspots;
- active incidents; and
- unusual changes in transportation conditions.

The platform should provide sufficiently fresh information to allow operational teams to identify and respond to emerging issues.

### 4.2 Business Analysts

Business Analysts use historical transportation data to identify trends, patterns, and performance indicators.

They require access to:

- historical trip data;
- route performance metrics;
- journey-time trends;
- congestion statistics;
- incident history;
- weather observations;
- geographical patterns; and
- aggregated mobility indicators.

The analytical datasets should be consistent, documented, and suitable for querying and visualization.

### 4.3 Management and Decision-Makers

Management requires high-level information to evaluate transportation performance and support strategic decisions.

They require metrics such as:

- overall network performance;
- route reliability;
- average journey times;
- congestion trends;
- incident impact;
- demand patterns; and
- performance changes over time.

The platform should allow management to identify areas requiring operational improvement or additional resources.

### 4.4 Data Engineering Team

The Data Engineering Team is responsible for maintaining the reliability and performance of the UrbanPulse data platform.

They require visibility into:

- pipeline execution status;
- ingestion volumes;
- processing duration;
- pipeline failures;
- retry attempts;
- data freshness;
- data-quality failures;
- rejected records;
- streaming latency; and
- system health.

The platform should provide sufficient observability to allow engineers to diagnose failures and identify potential data or infrastructure issues.

### 4.5 Data Consumers

Data consumers include downstream applications, dashboards, analytical tools, and potential machine-learning workflows that depend on trusted UrbanPulse datasets.

They require:

- reliable datasets;
- consistent schemas;
- documented fields;
- predictable data availability;
- appropriate historical coverage; and
- clearly defined data quality expectations.
